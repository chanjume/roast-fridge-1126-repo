One line (105 characters) roasting the smart fridge, entered in
[Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins](https://make.fun/competitions/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9)
on make.fun.

`main.py` writes the roast to `/output/entry.txt`:

> My smart fridge texted me at 2:14 a.m. that the oat milk expired — then ordered three more cartons of it.

The fridge nags about expired oat milk at 2:14 a.m., then reorders the
very thing it just declared dead — a specific, real smart-fridge
behaviour with a sting at the end.
